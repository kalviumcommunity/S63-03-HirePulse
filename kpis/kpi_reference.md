# KPI Reference Document

## Purpose

This document formally defines the business KPIs used for the HirePulse
customer analysis. Each KPI has a standardized definition, formula,
data source, target range, owner, update frequency, and interpretation
notes.

The primary dataset is:

`data/raw/customer_segment_data.csv`

Available columns:

- `customer_id`
- `customer_type`
- `product`
- `revenue`
- `churn`

The target ranges in this document are management/analysis targets
created for this assignment. They are not historical company targets.

---

# KPI 1: Average Revenue per Customer

**Definition:**  
Average revenue generated per unique customer.

**Formula:**  

`Total Revenue / Number of Unique Customers`

**Data Source:**  
`data/raw/customer_segment_data.csv`

**Columns:**  
`customer_id`, `revenue`

**Target Range:**  
`$80,000 - $90,000`

**Owner:**  
Finance / Business Analytics Lead

**Update Frequency:**  
Monthly

**Notes:**  
Measures average customer revenue and helps monitor the economic value
of the customer base. Higher values can indicate stronger customer
monetization, but should be interpreted together with churn and segment
mix.

---

# KPI 2: Customer Churn Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of customers marked as churned in the customer dataset.

**Formula:**  

`Churned Customers / Total Customers`

**Data Source:**  
`data/raw/customer_segment_data.csv`

**Columns:**  
`customer_id`, `churn`

**Target Range:**  
`0% - 15%`

**Owner:**  
Customer Success Lead

**Update Frequency:**  
Monthly

**Notes:**  
Indicates customer retention performance. A lower churn rate represents
better customer retention.

---

# KPI 3: Enterprise Revenue per Customer

**Definition:**  
Average revenue generated by each Enterprise customer.

**Formula:**  

`Enterprise Revenue / Enterprise Customer Count`

**Data Source:**  
`data/raw/customer_segment_data.csv`

**Columns:**  
`customer_type`, `customer_id`, `revenue`

**Target Range:**  
`$230,000 - $255,000`

**Owner:**  
Enterprise Sales Lead

**Update Frequency:**  
Monthly

**Notes:**  
Tracks monetization of Enterprise customers and can be used to monitor
changes in Enterprise customer value.

# KPI 6: Enterprise Revenue Share

**Definition:**  
Percentage of total revenue generated by Enterprise customers.

**Formula:**  

`Enterprise Revenue / Total Revenue`

**Data Source:**  
`data/raw/customer_segment_data.csv`

**Columns:**  
`customer_type`, `revenue`

**Target Range:**  
`65% - 80%`

**Owner:**  
Business Strategy Lead

**Update Frequency:**  
Monthly

**Notes:**  
Shows how dependent total revenue is on the Enterprise segment.
Changes in this KPI should be interpreted together with customer
concentration and churn.

# KPI Governance

## Definition Consistency

All teams should use the formulas documented in this reference file.
The KPI computation functions in `kpis/kpi_functions.py` provide the
standardized implementation.

## Target Governance

Target ranges are stored separately in:

`kpis/kpi_validation_targets.json`

This allows target values to be updated without changing the KPI
computation logic.

## Data Changes

If the underlying data schema changes:

1. Update the KPI reference document.
2. Update the corresponding computation function.
3. Update the target configuration if required.
4. Validate the KPI against historical results.
5. Document the definition change.
6. Restate historical results when required for consistent comparison.
