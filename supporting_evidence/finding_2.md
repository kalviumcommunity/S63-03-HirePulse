# Finding

Product-level churn is relatively similar across the available product lines.

## Supporting Evidence

- Dataset: `data/raw/customer_segment_data.csv`
- Metric: churned customers divided by customers in each `product`
- Product A: 6 of 51 churned, 11.76%
- Product B: 6 of 51 churned, 11.76%
- Product C: 5 of 48 churned, 10.42%
- Comparison: the highest-to-lowest difference is 1.34 percentage points.

## Why This Evidence Matters

The narrow spread means the evidence does not support making product line the
first retention segmentation. It helps prevent the team from directing effort
toward a product explanation that the current sample does not distinguish.

## Business Meaning

Retention planning should begin with customer type and support workload while
product teams continue monitoring the small observed differences.
