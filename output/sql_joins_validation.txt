JOIN DECISIONS
1. customers LEFT JOIN orders: preserves all customers for customer-level reporting.
2. orders LEFT JOIN customers with IS NULL: identifies orphaned orders.
3. customers LEFT JOIN customer_features: adds customer behavior metrics while preserving customer-order rows.
4. INNER JOIN is used for matched customer-order analysis.
5. SQLite FULL OUTER JOIN is emulated using LEFT JOIN + UNION ALL.
